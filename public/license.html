DIRECTIVE 01
CREDIT THE SOURCE
You must credit CIGA SYSTEMS and GaboBars when your work is based upon the CIGA universe. If your work also uses original CIGA-related material created by another contributor, that contributor must receive appropriate credit as well.
DIRECTIVE 02
DERIVATIVE WORKS MUST REMAIN WITHIN CIGA
A creator may use, adapt or build upon another creator's CIGA-related work when the resulting work is itself created as part of, or in connection with, the CIGA SYSTEMS universe. For example, a person may create a game based upon another person's CIGA game, provided that the new project remains a CIGA-related work. This permission does not authorize the extraction and reuse of a CIGA contributor's original characters, stories, artwork or other creations in an unrelated project outside the CIGA creative universe.

SHARE DERIVATIVE WORKS
If you create an original work based upon CIGA SYSTEMS or upon another CIGA-related work, other creators must be permitted to use that work as a foundation for further CIGA-related creative works, subject to the terms of this license. This permission applies specifically to further creation within the CIGA SYSTEMS universe and does not grant unrestricted permission to reuse the work outside that context.

CONTENT LIMITATIONS
CIGA-related works may contain mature themes, horror, violence, crime and other dark fictional material. However, works must not contain or promote prohibited extreme material, including sexual content involving minors, sexual exploitation of minors, or comparable illegal exploitative material. Creators are responsible for ensuring that their own works comply with applicable law and platform rules.
DIRECTIVE 05
CIGA IDENTIFIER
The official CIGA SYSTEMS logo must not be altered, redesigned, distorted, recolored or otherwise modified when used as an official identifier of the CIGA universe. Independent artwork may depict, reference or interpret CIGA concepts without modifying the official logo.
DIRECTIVE 06
NON-COMMERCIAL PROJECTS
Complete derivative projects such as games, standalone websites, films, animations or similar substantial works based upon CIGA SYSTEMS may not be sold or commercially distributed as paid products. This restriction does not prevent creators from independently offering commissioned creative services. For example, an artist may charge a client for creating commissioned CIGA-related artwork. The payment is for the creator's service and does not transfer ownership of CIGA SYSTEMS.
DIRECTIVE 07
NO FALSE OWNERSHIP
Contributors may not claim that they created the original CIGA SYSTEMS universe or that they are the original owner of CIGA SYSTEMS. Contributors may claim authorship of their own original additions and creations. Derivative works must remain identifiable as independent works created by their respective authors.
DIRECTIVE 08
PRESERVE CREATOR CREDIT
When using another creator's CIGA contribution, reasonable and visible attribution must be preserved. A creator may not remove, hide or intentionally misrepresent another contributor's authorship in order to present that contribution as their own.

Creator A creates an original character for a CIGA project. Creator B may use that character in a new CIGA story or project, provided that Creator A receives appropriate credit.
